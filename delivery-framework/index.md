## Framework Overview

This portfolio section presents selected evidence from a structured end-to-end Power BI delivery framework covering the complete analytics lifecycle from initial business problem through requirements, design, implementation, validation, deployment and operational support.

The framework demonstrates capability in:

- Business analysis and requirements engineering
- BPMN process modelling
- Analytics delivery governance
- Data and solution design
- Power BI development lifecycle management
- Testing and validation
- Task-to-document traceability
- Documentation and artefact management
- Deployment and operational readiness

The complete framework contains detailed subprocess models, document associations and reusable delivery documentation.

Only selected portfolio evidence is published here. Full process definitions, editable diagrams, association data and reusable templates are retained privately.
