## Project Overview

This showcase demonstrates the end-to-end delivery of a Power BI analytics solution designed to improve visibility of governance approval requests and identify items requiring attention.

The project is presented as a practical case study showing how business analysis, data preparation, semantic modelling, report development, validation and governance were applied to solve a defined business problem.
