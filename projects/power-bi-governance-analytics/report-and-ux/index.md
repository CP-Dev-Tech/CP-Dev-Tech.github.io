## Overview

This section presents selected evidence of how the analytical model was translated into a usable Power BI reporting experience.

It demonstrates how report pages, visuals, interactions and navigation were designed to support both operational monitoring and higher-level governance insight.

## What This Demonstrates

* Report-page design
* Visual selection
* Information hierarchy
* KPI presentation
* Slicer and filter design
* Interaction and navigation design
* Operational and management reporting
* Usability considerations
* Accessibility considerations
* Alignment of report design to business requirements

## Evidence Presented

Selected portfolio evidence will include:

* Report-page structure
* Representative dashboard screenshots
* KPI and visual design
* Filter and slicer behaviour
* Navigation and interaction design
* Selected UX decisions
* Examples of how requirements were translated into report outputs

Only selected report views are presented publicly. The complete PBIX implementation and reusable report-development artefacts are retained privately.
