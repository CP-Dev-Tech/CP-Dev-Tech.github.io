This section presents selected evidence of the business analysis and requirements work undertaken before the Power BI solution was designed and built.

* Desired business outcomes
* Project scope
* Business rules
* Functional requirements
* Non-functional requirements
* Success criteria
* Stakeholder analysis

Only selected requirements and document extracts are presented publicly. The complete Business Requirements Document and reusable document structure are retained privately.
