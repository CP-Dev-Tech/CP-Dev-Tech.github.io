# Delivery Frameworks

[← Back to Portfolio Home](/)

This section presents structured delivery frameworks that support consistent analysis, design, implementation, validation and governance across technology and analytics projects.

The frameworks are intended to demonstrate how I structure and control end-to-end delivery, rather than simply showing isolated project outputs.

## Power BI End-to-End Delivery Framework

A structured framework covering the Power BI analytics lifecycle from initial business problem through requirements, data preparation, semantic modelling, report development, validation, deployment and operational support.

The framework includes:

- End-to-end process modelling
- Detailed BPMN subprocesses
- Defined activities and decision points
- Document and artefact structures
- Task-to-document associations
- Requirements and validation traceability
- Delivery governance
