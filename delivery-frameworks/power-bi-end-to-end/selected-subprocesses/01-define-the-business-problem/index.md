# 01 — Define the Business Problem

[← Selected Subprocesses](/delivery-frameworks/power-bi-end-to-end/selected-subprocesses/) · [Power BI Framework Overview](/delivery-frameworks/power-bi-end-to-end/)

## Purpose

This subprocess demonstrates how an analytics initiative begins by establishing a clear and evidence-based understanding of the business problem before requirements, data or solution design activities begin.

The objective is to understand **why change is required**, who is affected and what business outcomes need to be achieved without prematurely defining the technical solution.

## What This Demonstrates

This subprocess provides evidence of:

* structured business-problem analysis;
* current-state investigation;
* stakeholder engagement;
* identification of operational and decision-making impacts;
* definition of desired business outcomes;
* separation of the business problem from the proposed technical solution;
* controlled decision points and validation; and
* progression into the next stage of requirements definition.

## Relationship to the End-to-End Framework

**Define the Business Problem** is the first substantive stage of the Power BI End-to-End Delivery Framework.

Its outputs provide the business context required by subsequent activities, particularly:

**02 — Define Data Requirements**

This ensures that data requirements are derived from a defined business need rather than being driven solely by the availability of source data or by assumptions about the intended report.
