# Selected Subprocesses

[Home](/) · [Delivery Frameworks](/delivery-frameworks/) · [Power BI End-to-End](/delivery-frameworks/power-bi-end-to-end/) · **Selected Subprocesses**

## Overview

The complete Power BI End-to-End Delivery Framework contains detailed subprocess models beneath the applicable stages of the high-level lifecycle.

Rather than publishing the complete subprocess library, this portfolio presents a representative selection demonstrating how different types of delivery activity are decomposed into controlled tasks, decisions, validation points and remediation loops.

The selected examples span business analysis, data preparation, analytical modelling, report development and validation.

## Selected Examples

### 01 — Define the Business Problem

Demonstrates how an analytics initiative begins with structured investigation of the business problem, affected stakeholders, current-state impacts and intended outcomes before solution design begins.

[Explore subprocess →](01-define-the-business-problem/)

### 03 — Design Representative Test Scenarios

Demonstrates how expected behaviour and representative business scenarios are defined early enough to support later transformation, modelling and end-to-end validation.

[Explore subprocess →](03-design-representative-test-scenarios/)

### 05 — Build Power Query Transformations

Demonstrates how approved data requirements and source design are translated into controlled data-preparation and transformation activities.

[Explore subprocess →](05-build-power-query-transformations/)

### 07 — Define the Semantic Model

Demonstrates how analytical requirements are translated into model structure, grain, relationships, measures and supporting semantic-model design decisions.

[Explore subprocess →](07-define-the-semantic-model/)

### 13 — Build Report Pages

Demonstrates how approved analytical requirements, model capabilities and report-design decisions are translated into usable Power BI report pages.

[Explore subprocess →](13-build-report-pages/)

### 18 — Perform End-to-End Source-to-Report Testing

Demonstrates how the completed solution is validated across the full analytical chain from source data through transformation, semantic modelling, calculation logic and final report output.

[Explore subprocess →](18-perform-end-to-end-source-to-report-testing/)

## What the Examples Demonstrate

Together, these subprocesses show how the framework:

* decomposes high-level lifecycle stages into executable tasks;
* introduces decisions and validation at appropriate points;
* links business requirements to technical delivery;
* incorporates remediation rather than assuming first-time success;
* maintains continuity between analysis, implementation and testing; and
* connects process activities to controlled project documentation.

## Portfolio Evidence Boundary

These subprocesses are representative examples only.

The complete framework contains substantially more detailed subprocess models covering the remaining stages of the delivery lifecycle.

For the published examples:

* diagrams are provided only as flattened, watermarked portfolio images;
* selected task-to-document associations may be shown to demonstrate traceability;
* reusable document templates are not published; and
* editable BPMN or diagram source files remain private.

The complete subprocess library, source models and association data are retained privately.
