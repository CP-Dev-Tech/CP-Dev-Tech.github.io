# Power BI End-to-End Delivery Framework

[Home](/) · [Projects](/projects/) · [Delivery Frameworks](/delivery-frameworks/) · **Power BI End-to-End**

## Overview

The **Power BI End-to-End Delivery Framework** is a reusable delivery structure for taking an analytics initiative from an identified business problem through requirements, data preparation, semantic modelling, reporting, validation, deployment and operational support.

It is designed to connect **business analysis, data analytics and Power BI delivery** within one controlled lifecycle rather than treating requirements, technical implementation, testing and documentation as separate activities.

The framework provides:

* a defined end-to-end delivery process;
* detailed subprocesses beneath applicable lifecycle stages;
* a controlled document and artefact structure;
* task-to-document traceability;
* requirements-to-implementation-to-validation traceability;
* explicit validation and remediation loops; and
* structured transition into deployment and operational support.

## Framework Structure

At a high level, the framework connects:

**Business Problem & Requirements**

↓

**Data Requirements, Source Design & Preparation**

↓

**Semantic-Model Design & Implementation**

↓

**Report Design & Implementation**

↓

**Validation & Remediation**

↓

**Deployment & Release**

↓

**Operational Support & Monitoring**

Controlled documentation and traceability operate across the lifecycle rather than being added only at the end.

## Design Principles

The framework is based on several practical principles:

* start with the business problem rather than the proposed technology;
* derive data and analytical requirements from defined business needs;
* define representative test scenarios and expected results early;
* separate design decisions from implementation;
* validate data and analytical behaviour progressively throughout delivery;
* document material technical decisions where they are made;
* maintain traceability between requirements, design, implementation and evidence;
* treat remediation as part of the controlled process;
* distinguish functional correctness from performance optimisation;
* prepare deployment and operational support before production release; and
* scale documentation and controls according to the complexity and risk of the project.

## Business Analysis and Data Analytics

Although the framework covers the complete Power BI delivery lifecycle, its primary purpose within this portfolio is to demonstrate the connection between **Business Analysis and Data Analytics**.

It shows how business problems and stakeholder needs can be translated into:

* testable requirements;
* defined data needs;
* controlled analytical logic;
* semantic-model decisions;
* meaningful reporting;
* validation criteria; and
* evidence that the delivered solution addresses the original requirement.

The technical breadth shown across data extraction, transformation, modelling, Power BI implementation, security and operations demonstrates the level of technical understanding needed to work effectively with developers, data engineers, architects and other specialists across an end-to-end analytics solution.

It is not intended to represent specialist expertise in every engineering or development discipline.

## Reusable but Adaptable

The framework provides a reusable structure rather than requiring every project to use the same technical solution or identical level of documentation.

A project should apply the relevant activities and controls according to:

* business complexity;
* data complexity;
* analytical risk;
* security requirements;
* implementation scale;
* delivery environment; and
* operational needs.

This allows the framework to provide consistency without creating unnecessary process or documentation.

* complete task-to-document association data;
* reusable Markdown document templates;
* detailed framework implementation guidance;
* controlled test and validation assets; and
* project-specific working documentation.
