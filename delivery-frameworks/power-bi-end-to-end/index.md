# Power BI End-to-End Delivery Framework

[Home](/) · [Projects](/projects/) · [Delivery Frameworks](/delivery-frameworks/) · **Power BI End-to-End**

## Framework Overview

This section presents selected evidence from a structured end-to-end Power BI delivery framework covering the complete analytics lifecycle from initial business problem through requirements, design, implementation, validation, deployment and operational support.

The framework demonstrates capability in:

- Business analysis and requirements engineering
- BPMN process modelling
- Analytics delivery governance
- Data and solution design
- Power BI development lifecycle management
- Testing and validation
- Task-to-document traceability
- Documentation and artefact management
- Deployment and operational readiness

## Framework Structure

The complete framework contains:

- A high-level end-to-end delivery process
- Detailed BPMN subprocesses
- Defined tasks, decisions and process flows
- Controlled document and artefact templates
- Task-to-document associations
- Section-level traceability
- Requirements and validation alignment
- Delivery governance across the lifecycle

## Portfolio Evidence

Selected examples will be presented to demonstrate:

- The overall end-to-end process
- Representative subprocess diagrams
- How individual activities are defined
- How process tasks consume and update project documentation
- Task-to-document and section-level associations
- How the framework was applied to a practical showcase project

The complete framework, editable process models, association data and reusable document templates are retained privately.
